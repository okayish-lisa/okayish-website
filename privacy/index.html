1. Our Promise to You

Legal version

Purpose and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Larian Group Inc., a corporation based in Ontario, Canada ("Larian," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with the Okay*ish mobile application and related services (the "App").

The App provides AI-generated comedic reframing of everyday moments. It is designed on a data-minimization principle: the text you enter describing your situation ("Your Words") is used only to generate your response and is not stored by us in any database, server log, analytics system, crash report, or on your device.

This policy applies to all users of the App, on any platform or device. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

Plain language

The Short Version

Okay*ish helps you get through a hard moment by finding the funny side of it. We built it with one firm rule:

We never keep what you type. Your words are used to write your kit, then they're gone from Okay*ish. Nothing you type is ever tied to your account.

The only thing we remember is what makes you laugh, and even that is kept as numbers, not a story.

There is one honest exception, and we explain it in Section 4: the AI company that writes your kit may hold onto a request for a short time for safety checks. We would rather tell you that up front than make a promise we can't fully keep.

2. Information We Collect

Legal version

Categories of Information

We collect and store only the following categories of information:

Preference Totals: Running totals that record which reframe lenses and comedic styles have worked for you, grouped by broad situation type. Situation types and styles are stored as numeric codes, not words. We do not keep a record of individual visits, the time or day of your visits, or any text. Preference totals are stored on your device. If you have an account, a synchronized copy is stored on our servers under your account identifier, which is a randomly generated code and does not contain your name or email address.

Settings: The voice setting you choose (such as Friendly, Snarky, or Unfiltered).

Usage Count: The number of free uses you have used, linked to a randomly generated identifier, so we can apply the free-use limit described in our Terms.

Account Information (optional): Signing in is optional. If you create an account, we collect the email address you use to sign in. Sign-in works by sending a one-time code to that email address. If you have an account, we also record whether you have agreed to receive marketing emails, as described in Section 6.

Subscription Information: If you subscribe, payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive your subscription status, the plan purchased, and renewal and expiry dates. We never receive your card or bank details.

Feedback You Choose to Send: If you send feedback from within the App, we receive the information described in Section 5.

Support Correspondence: If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Technical Information: Our servers and service providers process limited technical information needed to deliver the App and keep it secure, such as IP address, device type, operating system, and app version. This information is never combined with Your Words.

Plain language

What We Actually Keep

Think of it like a barista who remembers you like oat milk but has no idea what you talked about on the phone while waiting in line. We know your taste. We don't know your story.

Here is the whole list:

Your style scores: Numbers that say things like "for situation type 3, the dry style worked most of the time." Even the situation type is a number, not a word. These live on your phone. If you have an account, a backup copy is kept on our servers so they can follow you to a new phone. The backup is filed under your account ID, which is a random code, not your name or email.

Your settings: Which voice you picked.

How many free uses you've had: So the app knows when your free uses are done.

Your email, if you sign up: Signing up is optional. Your email is for logging in. We only send you news or offers if you say yes (see Section 6).

Your subscription status: Whether you're subscribed and when it renews. Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see your card.

Anything you choose to send us: Feedback you send from the app, or an email to support.

Basic technical details: Like what kind of phone you have, so the app works and stays secure. Never connected to what you type.

There is nothing in any of this that says what actually happened in your day.

3. Information We Do Not Collect or Store

Legal version

Excluded Data Categories

The following information is excluded from collection, storage, logging, or any other form of persistence by Larian:

Your Words: The text you enter is held in memory only for the duration of the request needed to generate your response. It is not written to any database, file system, cache, server log, analytics event, error or crash report, or to storage on your device, and it is never linked to your account or identifier.

Generated Kit Content: Reframes, one-liners, song suggestions, and related text generated for you are not saved to our servers or to your device, and are never included in feedback you send. We do not keep a history of your kits.

Summaries or Derivatives: We do not create or retain summaries, paraphrases, keywords, or semantic representations of Your Words. The only derived data retained is the numeric situation-type code added to your Preference Totals.

Inferences: We do not store inferences about your emotional state, mental health, relationships, employment, or any other personal circumstance.

Advertising and Tracking: The App contains no advertising and no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking tools. We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share it for advertising purposes.

Device Data: We do not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Your Device's Keyboard: Text you type is entered through your device's keyboard, which is provided by your operating system or by a third-party keyboard you have installed. The keyboard is not part of the App. Your operating system's keyboard may learn words you type to improve autocorrect and suggestions, and third-party keyboards may collect what you type under their own privacy policies. These practices are outside our control. You can manage them in your device settings.

Plain language

What We Will Never Know About You

Whatever you type into Okay*ish is used to write your kit and then it's gone from Okay*ish. Not saved on our servers. Not saved on your phone. Not summarized. Not tied to your account.

Your kit isn't saved either. There's no history screen and no diary of your bad days. We don't keep notes about your boss, your argument, or your 3 a.m. spiral, not even a watered-down version.

No ads. No ad trackers. We don't sell your information to anyone, ever.

We don't ask for your location, your contacts, your photos, your camera, or your microphone. Okay*ish doesn't need them.

About your keyboard: Your phone's keyboard isn't part of Okay*ish. Like in any app, your phone's built-in keyboard may learn words you use so autocorrect works better. If you've installed a different keyboard app, that company has its own privacy rules. We can't control either one, but you can change keyboard settings on your phone anytime.

If someone looked at everything we have about you, they would see some numbers about what style lands for you, your settings, and maybe your email. That's it.

4. How Your Information Is Processed

Legal version

Processing Architecture

Generating Your Kit: When you submit Your Words, the App sends them through our application server, which runs on Supabase infrastructure in Canada, to our AI service provider, OpenAI, in the United States, together with your style preferences expressed in plain words for that single request. No account identifier, email address, name, or device identifier is included in the request. Our server passes Your Words to OpenAI and does not store or log them.

Classification and Safety: As part of the same request, the AI provider assigns your situation a weight (everyday, heavy, or crisis) and a broad situation type, and generates your response. The AI provider is instructed to check its response, within the same request, before it is shown to you. Where a situation is classified as a possible crisis, the App displays crisis resources instead of a comedic response.

Learning Signals: Only deliberate actions you take on a kit (such as keeping or passing on a piece, or copying a one-liner) update your Preference Totals. Inaction, closing the App, or starting a new moment is never recorded as a signal. Heavy and crisis moments do not generate signals.

AI Provider Retention: OpenAI processes Your Words to generate your response under its API data terms. Under those terms, OpenAI does not use API content to train its models by default, and may retain API requests for up to thirty (30) days for abuse and misuse monitoring before deletion, unless longer retention is required by law. This retention is outside our control. Requests are not linked to your identity by us. We will update this policy if a zero-retention arrangement is put in place.

Service Providers: We use the following categories of service providers, each bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations: OpenAI (AI generation, United States); Supabase (application server that relays requests to our AI provider, plus account, preference backup, usage count, and feedback storage, hosted in Canada); RevenueCat (subscription status, United States); Apple and Google (app distribution and payment); and email and hosting providers needed to operate the App and support.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect the rights or safety of any person, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to the information transferred.

Plain language

What Happens When You Use the App

Here is the full life of what you type, step by step:

1. You type what happened: The app sends it through our server, which runs on Supabase in Canada, to OpenAI, the AI company in the United States that writes your kit. Our server just passes it along. It doesn't save it. Along with it goes a short note about the style you like. Nothing that identifies you goes with it. No email, no name, no account ID.

2. The AI writes your kit: It also figures out how heavy the moment is and the general type of situation. If it looks like you might be in danger, you'll see crisis support info instead of jokes. The AI is also told to check its own kit before you see it, to catch anything mean.

3. You react, if you want to: Keeping a piece, passing on one, or copying a one-liner teaches the app what works for you. Doing nothing teaches it nothing. Closing the app is never counted as "that helped" or "that didn't."

4. Your words are gone from Okay*ish: We don't keep them.

The honest part: OpenAI may keep a request for up to 30 days to check for abuse, then delete it. They don't use it to train their AI, and they don't know who you are, because we don't send them anything that identifies you. We're working on removing even this, and we'll update this policy if we do.

The companies that help us run the app: OpenAI writes the kits. Supabase runs our server in Canada, which passes what you type to OpenAI without saving it. Supabase also stores the backup of your style scores, your account, and any feedback you send, in Canada. RevenueCat keeps track of subscriptions. Apple and Google handle downloads and payments. They're all required to protect your information.

5. Sending Feedback

Legal version

Feedback

The App includes a Share feedback option that lets you tell us about anything in the App, including a kit piece you found hurtful, inappropriate, or wrong. Sending feedback is always optional and happens only at your direction.

Feedback may include an optional topic you select (such as "Too harsh" or "An idea"), an optional message you write, and an optional email address if you would like a reply. We ask that you not include names or other personal details in your message. If you do, they are stored as written.

If you choose to indicate that your feedback is about the kit you most recently received, the App also includes that kit's voice setting and its broad situation type (for example, "work"). The App shows you what will be included before you send it. The text of the kit and Your Words are never included in feedback.

To operate the feature, each submission also includes the App version, device model, and operating system version.

Feedback is not linked to your account. It is stored separately from preference data, accessible only to personnel designated by Larian, and used solely to respond to you and to review and improve the App. Feedback is retained only as long as needed for these purposes, then deleted. Any reply email address is used only to respond to you. Where feedback about a kit is used to improve the App's safety testing, it is first rewritten as an invented example.

Plain language

When You Send Feedback

Tap Share feedback at the bottom of the main screen to tell us anything: something that broke, an idea, a kit piece that felt hurtful or just wrong, or that you love it. It's always optional.

You can pick a topic, write a note, and add your email if you'd like a reply. Please leave out names and personal details. If you include them, we'll see them.

If your note is about the kit you just got, you can tick a box to include its style (like Snarky) and the general type of situation (like Work). You'll see exactly what's included before you send. Never the kit itself, and never what you typed.

Each note also carries a few basic details: your app version, what kind of phone you have, and its iOS version. Without an email, we have no idea who sent it.

We read feedback to make the app better and to reply if you asked us to. We keep it only as long as we need it for that, then delete it.

6. Emails From Us

Legal version

Marketing Emails

If you have an account, we will send you marketing emails about new features, offers, and updates to the App only if you have given your express consent, either by ticking the optional box when you create an account or by turning on the "Hear from the llama" setting in the App. This option is off by default. We record whether you have consented and the date and time of each change, as required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

You may withdraw your consent at any time by turning off the setting in the App or by using the unsubscribe link included in every marketing email. Unsubscribe requests are processed within ten (10) business days. Withdrawing consent does not affect your access to the App or your account.

Marketing emails are sent by Larian Group Inc. and are not based on Your Words, which we do not retain. We do not share your email address with third parties for their own marketing. Users who have not created an account do not receive marketing emails.

Service Messages: We may send emails that are necessary to operate your account or subscription, such as account, security, or legal notices. These are not marketing emails and may be sent regardless of your email preference.

Plain language

Hearing From the Llama

We only email you about new features or offers if you say yes. That means ticking the box when you create your account, or turning on "Hear from the llama" in Settings. It stays off unless you turn it on.

Changed your mind? Turn it off in Settings, or tap unsubscribe at the bottom of any email. Either way, the app works exactly the same.

These emails are never based on what you type. We don't have it, so we couldn't if we tried. And we never give your email to anyone else to market to you.

If you haven't signed up, we don't have your email, so you won't hear from us at all.

Now and then we may need to send you something important about your account, like a security or legal notice. Those aren't marketing.

7. Data Security

Legal version

Security Measures

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encryption of data in transit, encryption of stored data at rest with keys managed separately from the database, storage of preference data as numeric codes under a randomly generated account identifier rather than your name or email address, and access controls limiting access to personnel who need it to operate the App.

Our data-minimization design provides additional protection: Your Words cannot be exposed in a breach of our systems because we do not retain them.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we use commercially reasonable means to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach of security safeguards creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected users and regulators as required by law.

Plain language

How We Keep Your Information Safe

The little we do keep is encrypted, both while it travels and while it's stored. Your style scores are just numbers, filed under a random account ID rather than your name or email.

The real security advantage is simple: the most personal thing you share with us, what actually happened, can't be stolen from us because we don't have it. You can't leak what you don't keep.

No system is perfect. If something ever went wrong in a way that could hurt you, we would tell you, as the law requires.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Legal version

Retention Periods

Your Words: Not retained by Larian beyond the processing of the request. Retention by our AI provider is described in Section 4.

Preference Totals and Settings: Retained on your device until you reset them or delete the App, and on our servers until you reset them or delete your account or identifier.

Usage Count and Subscription Information: Retained while needed to provide the App and apply the free-use limit, and as required for legal, tax, and accounting purposes.

Account Information: Retained until you delete your account. Account deletion is available within the App's settings. Deleted data is removed from our active systems within thirty (30) days, and from backups on their normal cycle, except your email address and email preference records, which are kept as described below.

Email Preference: Your marketing email preference and the record of each choice you make (what you chose and when) are retained while you have an account. After you delete your account, we keep your email address and these records solely as proof of consent, for as long as needed to demonstrate compliance with applicable law. We do not use them for any other purpose, and we do not send marketing emails to deleted accounts.

Feedback: Retained only as long as needed to review it, respond to you, and improve the App, then deleted.

Support Correspondence: Retained as long as needed to resolve your request, then deleted.

Plain language

How Long We Keep Things

What you type: We don't keep it at all. (OpenAI may keep it up to 30 days, as explained in Section 4.)

Your style scores and settings: Until you reset them or delete your account. You can do both right in the app.

Your account, if you signed up: Until you delete it. Delete your account in settings and it's gone within 30 days, with one exception.

The exception, your email choices: If you ever said yes or no to emails, we keep your email address and a record of each choice, even after you delete your account. The law asks us to be able to prove we had your permission. We use it for nothing else, and we won't email you.

Feedback: Kept as long as we need it to fix things and reply to you, then deleted.

Support emails: Kept until we've sorted things out for you.

Subscription records: Kept as long as the law requires for business records.
