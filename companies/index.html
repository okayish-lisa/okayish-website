Why It Works

The benefit your team will actually reach for.

Most workplace wellness benefits go unused because they're either too clinical, too time-consuming, or feel like something HR will see. Okay*ish solves all three.

Fits into the real workday

A two-minute session during lunch is realistic. A 45-minute EAP call when you're swamped is not. Okay*ish is built for the former.

Genuinely private

No one at your company sees usage data, session content, or individual activity. Not HR, not management. And what people type isn't kept, so even we can't read it. This is what makes people actually use it.

Keeps people productive

Every unprocessed hard moment is lost focus. Okay*ish gives employees a quick, private way to work through it and get back to their day.

No clinical infrastructure required

Okay*ish doesn't require an onboarding program, manager training, or ongoing administration. Deploy it and let people find it when they need it.

A meaningful benefit signal

Offering Okay*ish signals that your company takes emotional wellbeing seriously, without requiring people to raise their hand and ask for help.

Works alongside your EAP

Okay*ish is not a replacement for professional mental health support. It's the first line, the thing you reach for before things escalate.
