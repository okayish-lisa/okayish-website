3. Description of Service

Legal version

Service Overview

The App uses artificial intelligence to generate comedic reframes of everyday situations you describe. For everyday moments, the App provides a "kit" consisting of a short reframe, a one-liner, and a song suggestion. For situations the App classifies as heavy, it provides a gentler response. For situations it classifies as a possible crisis, it provides crisis resources instead of a comedic response.

The App is intended for general wellbeing and lighthearted perspective only. It uses a third-party AI service provider to process your input and generate responses, and maintains preference totals to tailor its style to you over time, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Creating an account is optional. Some features, such as keeping your preferences when you change devices, may require an account.

We may add, change, or remove features at any time. We will provide reasonable notice of material changes to core functionality.

Plain language

What Okay*ish Does

You tell Okay*ish what happened, in your own words. It gives you back a kit: a funny new way to look at the moment, a one-liner you can text a friend, and a song that fits.

If what you share is heavy, like grief or a loss, you get something gentler instead of jokes. If it sounds like you might be in danger, you get crisis support info instead.

The app uses AI to write your kit, and it learns what makes you laugh over time. (Our Privacy Policy explains how, and what we do and don't keep.)

You don't need an account to use it. An account just lets your preferences follow you to a new phone.

We'll keep adding and improving things. If anything major changes, we'll let you know.
